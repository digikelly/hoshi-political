📊 How We Determine Our Forecasts

Our passage probability forecasts are informed political analysis — not random guesses. Each forecast is manually evaluated based on:

  • Committee Composition: Who chairs the committee, partisan breakdown, member voting history
  • Bill Progress: How far the bill has advanced (passed Senate, stuck in committee, etc.)
  • Bipartisan Support: Republican/Democrat crossover potential, business community backing
  • Fiscal Impact: Budget implications that may attract or deter support
  • Timing & Session Dynamics: Days remaining, competing priorities, leadership signals
