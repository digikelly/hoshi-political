💡 Key Takeaways
  • Housing wins: HB 2418 (permit reform) and SB 6026 (commercial zone flexibility) both passed, addressing two of Redmond's top priorities for development streamlining.
  • Tourism secured: HB 2325 passed, ensuring continued funding for destination marketing in Redmond.
  • Tax mix: Estate tax reduction (SB 6347) passed with bipartisan support, but the millionaires tax (SB 6346) also passed despite OneRedmond opposition.
  • Defeated threats: Employer assessment (SB 6173) and wealth tax (HB 1319) both failed, avoiding major new costs for Redmond businesses.
  • Amendment success: OneRedmond's advocacy secured a 40% retail cap amendment on SB 6026, protecting ground-floor retail requirements.

📅 Next Steps & Look Ahead

  • Governor Review: Passed bills await gubernatorial action (20-day window excluding Sundays)
  • SB 6346 Litigation: Expected legal challenge to the capital gains tax; monitor for budget implications
  • 2027 Session Prep: Begin identifying priorities for next session (January 2027)
  • Interim Work: Track implementation of HB 2418 (permit reform) at local level
