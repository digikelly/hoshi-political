Executive Summary

HB 2325 establishes a tourism self-supported assessment program to fund statewide tourism promotion, creating a dedicated revenue stream for destination marketing. The bill has successfully cleared the March 2 fiscal cutoff and is currently on the House Floor awaiting a vote.

What This Bill Does

HB 2325 creates a dedicated funding mechanism for statewide tourism promotion through a self-supported assessment program. Key provisions include:

  • Establishes assessment-based funding for tourism marketing
  • Creates dedicated revenue stream for destination promotion
  • Supports statewide tourism industry development
Legislative Context

HB 2325 is part of a package of tourism-related bills moving through the Legislature. Its companion bill, SB 6061, has also cleared fiscal committees and is on the Senate Floor. Both bills aim to establish sustainable funding for Washington's tourism promotion efforts.

The bill aligns with HB 2278 (Tourism Promotion Areas), which also cleared the fiscal cutoff and is currently in House Rules.

Next Steps

With the fiscal cutoff behind us, HB 2325 is positioned for a House floor vote. The bill has already passed its fiscal committee and faces no further procedural hurdles before a final vote. Monitor for scheduling of the floor vote in the coming days.
