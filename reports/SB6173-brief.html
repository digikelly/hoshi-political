SB 6173
Apple Health Employer Assessment
● IN COMMITTEE
🚨 CRITICAL IMPACT
📍 Senate Ways & Means
Executive Summary

SB 6173 creates a new employer assessment to fund Apple Health (Medicaid) expansion. The bill targets employers with 100+ employees who have workers enrolled in Apple Health. This represents a significant new cost for large employers and could affect labor costs, hiring decisions, and benefits strategies. Executive session scheduled for February 19, 2026 at 4:00 PM.

🚨 CRITICAL CLIENT ALERT

This bill directly impacts employers with 100+ employees in Washington. The assessment could add significant annual costs depending on workforce composition and Apple Health enrollment. Companies should model potential costs and consider benefits strategies to minimize exposure.

Key Provisions
  • Target Employers: Those with 100+ employees statewide
  • Assessment Trigger: Employees enrolled in Apple Health
  • Assessment Amount: TBD - per employee per month
  • Effective Date: January 1, 2027 (proposed)
  • Administration: Department of Labor & Industries
  • Reporting: Quarterly employer reporting required

Strategic Analysis

Political Context: The bill addresses Medicaid funding gaps while shifting costs to large employers. Democrats view this as a fairness issue - large employers benefit from a healthy workforce while some employees rely on public health programs. Business groups strongly oppose, arguing it's a hidden tax on employment.

Implementation Challenges: If passed, L&I will need to build new reporting infrastructure. Employers will need systems to track Apple Health enrollment among employees. Multi-state employers face complexity determining which employees count toward the 100-employee threshold.

Competitive Impact: Washington employers would face additional costs not borne by competitors in other states. This could influence location decisions for expanding companies.

Client Action Items
  • Immediate: Contact Senate Ways & Means members before Feb 19 executive session
  • Cost Modeling: Estimate potential assessment based on current Apple Health enrollment
  • Benefits Review: Assess whether enhanced employer benefits could reduce Apple Health enrollment
  • Coalition Building: Join business association advocacy efforts against the bill
  • Contingency Planning: Model budget impacts if bill passes
  • Tracking Systems: Prepare to implement enrollment tracking if bill becomes law
