HB 2418 OneRedmond Active ✓ PASSED
Permit Review Process Reform
Housing / Permitting
+
Description
Establishes 120-day shot clocks for permit approvals, creates appeal process for delays, and prohibits cities from requesting endless "additional information." This bill directly addresses Redmond's development timeline challenges.
Final Vote
House: 78-20 | Senate: 38-11 | Delivered to Governor: March 13, 2026
OneRedmond Position
SUPPORT — Redmond has some of the longest permit timelines in King County. This bill forces faster processing or exposes the city to legal challenges. This was a top priority for Redmond business community.

SB 6026 OneRedmond Active ✓ PASSED
Residential Development in Commercial Zones
Housing / Zoning
+
Description
Removes ground-floor retail mandates for residential development in commercial zones. Amended version caps retail requirements at 40% of eligible areas with height incentives up to 85 feet.
Final Vote
Senate: 29-20 | House: 55-43 (amended) | Delivered to Governor: March 13, 2026
OneRedmond Position
MONITOR WITH AMENDMENTS — Supported increased housing density but raised walkability concerns. Successfully secured 40% retail cap amendment which was ACCEPTED in final version.
