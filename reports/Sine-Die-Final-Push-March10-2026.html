💰 BUDGET CRISIS & THE INCOME TAX GAMBLE

The Structural Problem: Washington's tax system is the most regressive in America. We rely on sales tax (7-10.5%) and B&O tax, which hammer low-income residents while wealthy residents pay virtually no income tax. The state faces recurring revenue shortfalls because this system doesn't capture wealth.

SB 6346 (Millionaires Tax) — The Revenue Solution:

  • What it does: 1% tax on capital gains over $250K ($500K joint filers)
  • Revenue: $500-800 million annually for education/early learning
  • Status: House Finance took exec action Feb 27 — advancing to floor
  • OneRedmond Position: OPPOSED (affects wealthy business owners, investors)
⚖️ THE COURT GAMBLE: If SB 6346 passes, it will be challenged immediately. Washington's Constitution (Article VII, Section 1) requires taxes to be "uniform upon the same class of property." The Supreme Court struck down an income tax in 1933 and has upheld that precedent for 90 years.

The Strategy: Democrats are calling this an "excise tax on capital asset sales" rather than income. If courts buy that framing, it creates $500M+ in new revenue. If courts reject it, the tax dies and the budget loses that funding — but Democrats can say they tried to tax the wealthy.

Budget Reality: Without SB 6346, the Legislature must either cut education funding, raise sales/B&O taxes (hitting everyone), or raid the rainy day fund. The budget writers are likely treating this revenue as "aspirational" — they'll account for it but have backup plans if courts kill it.

🎯 ONE REDMOND TRACKED BILLS — FINAL STATUS

🔴 CRITICAL: HB 2418 — Permit Review Reform (78% PASSAGE)

Status: Senate Floor, 2nd Reading | Melissa Stuart's Bill | Direct Redmond Impact

What it does: 120-day shot clocks for permit approvals, creates appeal process for delays, prohibits endless "additional information" requests from cities.

Why it matters for Redmond: Redmond has some of the longest permit timelines in King County. This bill forces the city to process permits faster or face legal challenges. It passed the House 78-20 with bipartisan support and is ready for Senate final passage.

Outlook: NEAR-CERTAIN PASSAGE — Floor vote expected Wednesday/Thursday.

🟡 HIGH PRIORITY: SB 6346 — Millionaires Tax (65% PASSAGE)

Status: House Finance → Floor | OneRedmond OPPOSED | Budget Critical

The Politics: House Democrats have the votes. This is their top revenue priority. It will pass the House this week.

The Legal Risk: 50/50 chance it survives court challenge. If struck down (likely), budget loses $500M+ and faces mid-year cuts or special session.

Client Impact: Affects high-net-worth individuals, business owners with equity, investors. Estate planning complexity increases.
