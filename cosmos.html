The Vision

Project COSMOS represents more than a performance—it is a global inflection point where the space industry and artistic expression converge. Born in Redmond, home to both SpaceX satellite operations and the innovative spirit of the Pacific Northwest, this collaboration will demonstrate that the final frontier belongs to all of humanity.

When a Starship dancer floats weightlessly above a Marymoor audience, when Tesla coils illuminate performers recreating the Big Bang, when the story of human space exploration is told through movement rather than machinery—we will have created something the world has never seen.

Performance Concepts
  • ◆
    The Space Walk
    Zero-gravity choreography using advanced wire systems and drone lighting to simulate EVA
  • ◆
    Ignition Sequence
    Tesla coil orchestra performing alongside dancers as Falcon Heavy lifts off on screen
  • ◆
    Starship Earth
    Massive inflatable Starship set piece that "launches" with performers inside
  • ◆
    The Overview Effect
    360° dome projection showing Earth from space while aerialists orbit above audience
  • ◆
    Mars Settlement Ballet
    Red-hued performance depicting the first colony, with Raptor engine sound design
